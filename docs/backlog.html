Initial project backlog

A proposed work plan for refining a detachable white-cane attachment. Priorities, individual ownership and acceptance thresholds will be agreed by the team and supervisor as requirements are developed.

Define the proposal scope
Project description, team details and initial literature review.Clarify user needs
Document upper-body obstacle scenarios and feedback preferences; arrange any further conversations with appropriate consent.Specify measurable requirements
Agree detection conditions, response time, false-alert criteria, size, weight, battery and cost targets.Compare sensing options
Record ultrasonic and time-of-flight trade-offs, component quotations and practical experiments.Investigate mounting and cane motion
Evaluate sensor orientation, attachment stability and whether inertial measurements can improve alerts.Prototype feedback patterns
Compare understandable distance-dependent haptic and/or audible cues.Define evaluation scenarios
Plan controlled tests for detection, misses, false alerts and latency, with a simple threshold baseline.Develop system analysis and design
Document architecture, interfaces, power budget, risks and an initial demonstration plan.Assess possible extensions
Review feasibility of object-type recognition and stair or drop-off detection after the core warning function.
